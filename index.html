Regime-Aware Factor Model

Markets don't behave the same way all the time, so one model rarely works everywhere. I used a Hidden Markov Model to label each month as bull, bear, or volatile, then used a different stock strategy for each.

Over 22 years of S&P 500 data, it returned 18.5% a year against the index's 12.9%, with a Sharpe ratio of 1.10 against 0.90.
